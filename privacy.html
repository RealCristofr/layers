gigly Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This explains what gigly collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in readable form). When you sign up we also record that you confirmed you're 18 or older and agreed to our terms, and when.
  • Profile: what you choose to add, including username, roles, city, tagline, bio, genres, influences, who you're looking for, and availability. Your city is whatever you type. We don't track your GPS location.
  • Audio: demos and layers you record or upload.
  • Messages and activity: messages with people you're connected with, connection requests, follows, blocks, and whether a message was delivered or read.
  • Reports: reports you send, including the reason and any details you add.
  • Device permissions: the microphone, only when you record, and your files, only when you choose a file to upload.

How we use it

To run gigly: sign you in, show your profile, play your demos, deliver messages, suggest musicians to you, and keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and stopping abuse. We don't use your information for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Signed-in users can see your profile and demos, unless you turn on Hide my profile or block them.
  • With Hide my profile on, you're hidden from Discover and Home, but people you're connected with can still see you.
  • Only you and the person you're chatting with can read your messages. Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, so we can technically access them. We only look when needed to handle a report, keep gigly safe, or follow the law.
  • Reports are private. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Who we share it with

Our hosting, database, authentication, and file-storage provider, Supabase, processes data for us so the app works. When we send email, we'll use an email delivery provider. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. We don't sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, demos, and messages within 30 days. Copies in routine backups expire on a regular schedule. We may keep limited records, such as reports, where needed for safety or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove profile information, demos, and layers in the app.
  • Hide your profile, or block and report anyone, in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing [contact email]. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

We use access controls so users can only read what they're allowed to. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Age limit

gigly is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we'll delete it if we learn we have.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
